Why free BPM tools still matter

Guide overview · Full article linked below

Tempo is the shared language of DJs, dancers, and producers. When you only need a quick read of an MP3 or WAV, installing a full digital audio workstation is slow. Browser-based BPM detection analyzes audio locally with Web Audio APIs so your unreleased demos do not have to leave your device.

In the full guide we explain how confidence scores work, when half-time and double-time buttons help, how tap tempo saves ambient tracks, and how to use a free BPM result when you prepare a setlist or a practice loop. The writing is original to this site and written for people who want clarity — not marketing hype.

Independent streaming for original music

Guide overview · Full article linked below

Major distributors are useful, but they are not the only path. Independent artists can host free listen experiences, publish an ISRC-aware catalog page, and still list on Spotify or YouTube Music when they choose. The important part is knowing what you control: masters, alternate free streams, and how you talk to listeners.

Our streaming article covers free public players, why content-addressed files help integrity, how to think about live rooms (Kick, Rumble, Twitch), and how optional support links (such as PayPal) can sit beside free access without forcing a paywall.
