About the author

Justin Helmer · Excavationpro · Lightfather

I am Justin Helmer, an independent musician and tool builder publishing as Excavationpro. I write under DeepSeek Oracle to share clear, original guides about free music utilities, tempo detection, and independent streaming — without requiring a subscription to read the articles.

My background is hands-on production and public tool maintenance: free browser BPM detection at bpmfinder.ca, a free listen portal for original tracks at asiancoastline.com, and open documentation for people who want transparency about how free tools work. I also maintain a denser “systems hub” for power users who need downloads and network maps; that hub lives at /lygo-systems-hub.html so this main site can stay readable.

What this site is for

DeepSeek Oracle is a small publisher site. The goal is educational content first: long-form pages that answer real questions producers ask. Advertising (when approved) helps host free tools. Optional support via PayPal is never required to use the guides or free utilities.

Experience & expertise

  • Open-source oriented documentation and GitHub projects under DeepSeekOracle
  • Practical writing aimed at beginners and intermediate producers
